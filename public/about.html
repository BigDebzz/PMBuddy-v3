About PM Buddy

Project management for people who are not project managers

Most people managing projects never asked for the job. They just found themselves responsible for making something happen. PM Buddy was built for them.

Our Mission

To give every person managing a project the structure, clarity and confidence of a professional project manager. Before they can afford one, and before they know they need one.

Our Vision

A world where no project starts on vibes and hope. Where founders, team leads, and builders of all kinds know their goal, own their risks and speak about their work with clarity.

Why PM Buddy exists

Around the world, millions of founders, team leads, consultants, and community builders are running projects every day without the training, tools, or support that professional project managers have.

They are managing launches, campaigns, product builds, and community initiatives on WhatsApp, spreadsheets, and hope. When things go wrong. missed deadlines, scope creep, poor documentation, no one knowing who owns what. it is not because they are bad at their work. It is because they were never given the right structure.

PM Buddy gives them that structure. Without requiring a certification. Without the complexity of enterprise tools. Without the learning curve that makes people give up before they start.

What we believe

Structure should not be complicated

Good project management is not about frameworks and jargon. It is about clarity, accountability, and knowing what comes next.

Everyone deserves good tools

Enterprise PM software was built for enterprise teams. PM Buddy is built for the rest of us. solo builders, small teams, and growing organisations.

AI should guide, not replace

PM Buddy uses AI to surface what matters, flag risks, and generate documents. The decisions still belong to you.

Built for the underserved majority

PM Buddy was built for the growing majority of people who manage projects without the title. Founders, team leads, consultants and builders working in small teams around the world.
