Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

PM Buddy ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. What We Collect

When you use PM Buddy, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and role when you sign up
  • Project data — projects, campaigns, documents, milestones, risks, and team information you create inside PM Buddy
  • Usage data — pages you visit and actions you take, collected via Google Analytics 4
  • Authentication data — login session tokens managed by Supabase

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the PM Buddy platform
  • To send you notifications about your projects (milestones, risks, team updates)
  • To send account-related emails such as email verification and password reset
  • To understand how people use PM Buddy so we can make it better

3. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the third-party services that power PM Buddy:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (supabase.com)
  • Google Analytics — usage analytics, only if you accept cookies (analytics.google.com)
  • Brevo — email delivery, for invitations, reminders and occasional product emails (brevo.com)
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — AI-powered features including the PM Buddy Assistant and document generation
  • Vercel — hosting and infrastructure (vercel.com)
  • Cloudflare — domain name and email forwarding (cloudflare.com)
  • Tally — the feedback form, loaded only when you press the feedback button (tally.so)

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers with row-level security enabled, meaning only you can access your own data. We use HTTPS for all data transmission. We do not store payment information.

Emails about your own projects, such as invitations and reminders, are sent as part of the service. A weekly summary of your projects with one tip, and occasional product updates, include an unsubscribe link. You can also turn them off in Settings.

5. Cookies

PM Buddy uses cookies for two purposes:

  • Session cookies — to keep you logged in (essential, cannot be disabled)
  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics 4, to understand usage patterns. These are only used if you press Accept on the cookie bar. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set.

You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer of the home page at pmbuddy.app. Fonts are hosted on our own servers, so your browser does not contact Google to load them. The feedback form is provided by Tally and is only loaded when you press the feedback button.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your project data
  • Opt out of analytics tracking by using browser privacy settings or an ad blocker

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

8. Children

PM Buddy is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or a notice inside PM Buddy.
