# PM Buddy Brand Guidelines

Decisions made once. `DESIGN.md` turns these into rules.

## Personality
Warm, confident and clear. A capable guide for people who are not project managers. Premium, not corporate. Never loud for the sake of it, never timid.

## Colour
- A warm neutral base: page `#F7F5F2`, cards white, ink `#1E1919`. In dark: `#171412`, `#221E1B`, `#F6F2EC`.
- One electric blue accent, `#1F57F0` (dark mode `#356FEE`, with `#8DB1FF` for text), used for actions and selection.
- Green, amber and red mean status only.
- Colour is blended on the landing page: a soft sky behind the product, a horizon glow, and a thin four-colour line (`#E8501F`, `#FFB020`, `#1ED6A0`, `#2D6CF6`).

## Type
- Schibsted Grotesk for headings, heavy and tightly tracked. Albert Sans for text.
- Large, confident headlines. Sentence case. No tiny uppercase labels.

## Icons and imagery
- One line-icon family, no emoji, no symbol characters.
- Show the real product, drawn from real sample data, before any abstract artwork.

## Voice
Plain English, short sentences, no jargon, no hype.

## Modes
Light, Dark and Auto, always available, chosen per person. Light must read well on a projector.
